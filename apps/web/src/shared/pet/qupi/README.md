# Qupi mascot art

Qupi (qubit + puppy) is the mascot of the web app. The art lives here as stacked WebP layers.

## Provenance

The source picture was generated by the team with an AI image tool from the team's own prompt. It was then
cut into layers, cleaned (background removed, fur edges decontaminated) and given drawn eyelid and mouth
overlays. The eyelids, closed mouth and half open mouth are made programmatically from the source colors.

## Layers

All layers share one square canvas and stack at 0,0, back to front: `tail`, `body`, `ear-left`, `ear-right`,
`head`, `mark`, `eyelids`, `mouth-closed`, `mouth-half`.

- `<layer>@2x.webp` is 240x240 (for a 120 px display on dense screens), `<layer>.webp` is 120x120.
- `body` fades out softly at the bottom (it is a bust). `head` has open eyes and the open smiling mouth.
- `mark` is the small gold qubit symbol only (for a glow); it is removed from `head`.
- `eyelids`, `mouth-closed` and `mouth-half` are overlays that line up exactly with `head`.
- `rig.ts` exports `QUPI_LAYERS` and `QUPI_ORIGINS` (transform-origin per layer).
- Head, ears, mark and overlays share the head transform (rotate about the neck, up to 6 degrees); ears add up
  to 8 degrees about their roots, with no lift or drop; the tail rotates about its base, up to 4 degrees. Moving
  layers are oversized and feathered so these angles show no hole; going further needs the art rebuilt and checked.
- Total size of the WebP files is under 150 KB.

| Background removal        | rembg 2.0.67 with the BiRefNet general model               | MIT        |

| Inpainting under overlays | OpenCV (Telea)                                             | Apache-2.0 |
| Masks, drawing, export    | Pillow, NumPy (hand made polygons and ellipses, feathered) | HPND, BSD  |
